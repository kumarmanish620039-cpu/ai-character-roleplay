Last Updated: October 3, 2026

MKCreation ("we", "our", or "us") operates the AI Character Roleplay mobile application ("App").

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use our App.

1. Information We Collect

AI Character Roleplay does not require you to create an account and does not request access to your device's camera, microphone, location, contacts, photos, or other sensitive device permissions.

We do not intentionally collect personal information such as:

  • Name

2. Chat and Conversation Data

AI Character Roleplay is designed to provide AI character conversations without sending your chat messages to our servers.

Your conversations are processed locally on your device. We do not receive, collect, or store your conversation content on our servers.

3. Device Permissions

The App does not require sensitive device permissions to provide its core functionality.

4. Third-Party Services

The App does not intentionally share your personal information or conversation content with third-party services.

If third-party services are added to the App in the future, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.

5. Data Security

Because the App does not transmit your conversations or personal information to our servers, we do not maintain a server-side database of your conversations.

However, you are responsible for maintaining the security of your own device.

6. Children's Privacy

AI Character Roleplay is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from users through the App.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by updating the "Last Updated" date of this Privacy Policy.
